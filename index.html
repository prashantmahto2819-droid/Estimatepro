Features

Everything an estimate needs, in one place

Built from real office estimates: the same measurement layout, the same abstracts and the same signatures. Without the hours spent fixing Excel formulas.

Measurement sheets

No × L × B × D × factor, deductions, headings, sub-totals and "same as item" rows. The net quantity flows straight into the abstract.

Rate analysis, updated

CPWD and MPPWD analyses re-priced with current labour, material and machinery rates. Change one basic rate and every item follows.

Parts & general abstract

Part A, Part B and more, each with its own abstract, plus a general abstract with GST. Headers and signatures print on every sheet.

Non-schedule items

How it works

From drawing to submitted estimate in four steps

No training needed. If you have made an estimate in Excel, you already know how to use Estimate Pro.

Design or draw

Size the structure in the Design Studio, or draw walls, slabs and roads in the CAD & BIM Studio. Quantities flow into the estimate automatically.

Pick your items

Search by code or in plain words, even site words like gitti, sariya or khudai, across CPWD, MPPWD, Horticulture and your own SORs.

Enter measurements

Type dimensions the way you write them in the MB, or let the BIM model generate them. Deductions, steel and "same as item" quantities are handled for you.

Export everything

Download Excel (measurement, analysis, abstracts, BOQ), export the 3D model as IFC / GLB / OBJ, send the estimate to your WhatsApp, and capture site photos with GPS.

About us

Built on site, for the people on site

Estimate Pro is developed by Prashant Mahto, a civil engineer working as an Overseer at the SECL Gevra Project. Every day he saw engineers lose hours to the same tasks: copying SOR descriptions, re-typing measurements, fixing broken Excel formulas and rebuilding abstracts after a single change.

So he built the tool he wanted for himself: one place where measurements, rates, analyses and abstracts stay linked, and the final Excel comes out right the first time. The aim is simple: to reduce the day-to-day hassle of civil engineers and give them back time for the actual work.

“An estimate should take the time it takes to think, not the time it takes to type.”
