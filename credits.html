4. Models and pictures you add yourself

You can add your own 3D models (GLB / glTF), picture cut-outs, material pictures and a background picture to a drawing. These stay in your drawing and on your device. If a file comes from the web, its licence may require you to show the author's name wherever you publish a picture or video of your design. Estimate Pro helps you keep that record:

  1. Open the drawing in CAD Studio.
  2. Choose File ▾ → Credits for models and pictures…
  3. For each item, enter the author, the source link and the licence, and press Save.
  4. Press Copy credits text and paste the text under your picture, in the video description or on the drawing sheet.

You are responsible for using the files you add within the terms of their licences.
