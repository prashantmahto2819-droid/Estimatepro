Estimate Pro, operated by Vividbrandify · Developed by Prashant Mahto (prashantmahto2819@gmail.com) · Effective 27 November 2026 (updated: consent, mobile / WhatsApp)

1. Who we are

Estimate Pro (“the App”) is a web application for preparing civil-engineering estimates, measurement sheets, rate analyses, abstracts and BOQs. It is provided by Vividbrandify, owned by Prashant Mahto (together “we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, contact us at presidentsri@live.com or +91 74005 01000.

3. Your consent and WhatsApp messages

  • No account without consent. Before an account is created — by email, OTP on WhatsApp / email, or Google — the App asks you to agree to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service. If you do not agree, the sign-up request is not sent and no account is made.
  • What you agree to (required): storing and using your name, email, mobile (WhatsApp) number and your work to run your account, and sending you sign-in codes (OTP) and account messages on WhatsApp or email.
  • WhatsApp notifications (optional): news of new features, licence / trial reminders and tips are sent on WhatsApp only if you tick that box. It is off unless you choose it, and you can turn it off at any time from the WhatsApp updates switch in the App.
  • Accounts made by the super admin, and accounts made before this version: you are asked for consent the first time you sign in. If you do not agree, you are signed out.
  • Messages you ask for yourself, such as sending an estimate to your own WhatsApp, are sent only when you press the button.
  • When this policy changes in a way that needs fresh consent, the App asks you again.

4. Files you upload

When you upload an SOR in Excel, CSV or PDF, the file is read inside your own browser. Only the extracted item list (code, description, unit, rate) is saved to your account. The original file itself is not uploaded or stored.

5. Who can see your data

  • You can see and change your own estimates and uploaded SORs.
  • Other users cannot see your estimates, your uploaded SORs, your email or your account details. This is enforced by database security rules, not just by the screens.
  • The super admin (Vividbrandify / Prashant Mahto) can see the list of users (name, email, role, licence, last activity, device type) and can open any user’s estimates, for support and administration. The super admin can also create accounts, set a new password for a user and change licences.
  • Shared library rates: the built-in rate library and basic rates, and any updated rates entered by editors, are visible to all signed-in users. They are common reference data, not personal data.

6. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions. Data is stored in the South Asia (Mumbai, India) region.
  • Google: only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), through our own messaging server (Vivid Relay): delivers sign-in codes, the estimates you send to yourself, account messages and — if you agreed — update notifications. Only your number and the message are passed on.
  • Website host: serves the App’s pages and may keep standard access logs.
  • Public code libraries (jsDelivr, cdnjs and Google Fonts): your browser downloads program code and fonts from these networks, which see your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The App stores your sign-in session and a few small settings in your browser’s local storage so that you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your account and work for as long as your account exists, including after a trial or activation ends, so that you can still open and export your estimates. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, estimates and uploaded SORs within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something. Provider logs are kept for the providers’ standard periods.

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS encryption. Access to data is controlled by row-level security in the database. Each account can be signed in on one computer at a time, and signing in elsewhere signs the older computer out. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

10. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it; or
  • withdraw your consent: WhatsApp notifications can be turned off in the App at any time; withdrawing the required consent means closing your account.

Write to presidentsri@live.com from the email address on your account. You can export your estimates to Excel at any time from inside the App.

11. Grievances

If you have a complaint about how your data is handled, contact our grievance contact at presidentsri@live.com / +91 74005 01000. We aim to respond within 30 days.

12. Children

The App is a professional tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also show a notice in the App.
